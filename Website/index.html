The code

Seven lines every member learns before they learn a single setup.

忍静誘斬律鏡散

1 / 7

  1. 忍

    Patience nin

    The first move of the session is usually bait. Let it happen without you.

  2. 静

    Stillness sei

    Sitting flat through chop is a position. Doing nothing well is a skill.

  3. 誘

    The lure yū

    Where stops gather, price will visit. Mark the pool before someone takes it.

  4. 斬

    The cut zan

    When it's time, enter once and cleanly. Hesitation is a second entry at a worse price.

  5. 律

    Discipline ritsu

    Risk is decided before the click, never after it.

  6. 鏡

    The mirror kyō

    Your journal shows who you were today. Read it the way a stranger would.

  7. 散

    Letting go san

    Petals fall without complaint. Take the loss, close the chart, walk outside.

Every market has four seasons.

Price moves in cycles, the way a tree does. The horizon in each painting is the shape of that phase. Learn to tell which one you're standing in.

No. Membership is handled through the Discord. This site is part of it.
